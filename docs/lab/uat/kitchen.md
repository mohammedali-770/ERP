# UAT — Kitchen

**Run by:** kitchen staff who prepare orders daily
**Duration:** about two hours
**Requires:** lab kitchen printer, barcode reader, a colleague sending orders

Covers `PRN-001`..`PRN-014` from the kitchen's side, and the kitchen half of
`ACC-006`.

> The kitchen is the noisiest, most time-pressured surface in the branch. A slip
> that is merely *correct* is not enough — it has to be readable at a glance by
> someone with wet hands.

---

## Part 1 · Reading the slip

| # | Task | Passes when |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Read an incoming slip and state what to make | Correct, without re-reading |
| 1.2 | Read a slip with modifiers — no onions, extra sauce | The modifier is **impossible to miss** |
| 1.3 | Read a slip with a customer note | Note stands out from the items |
| 1.4 | Read slips for delivery, pickup and dine-in | You can tell which is which immediately |
| 1.5 | Read a slip in Arabic | Correct layout; nothing truncated |

## Part 2 · Changes and cancellations

Where kitchens lose money and tempers.

| # | Task | Passes when |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | A change slip arrives for an order you started | Obvious it is a change, not a new order |
| 2.2 | Say what changed on that order | Clear without comparing two slips side by side |
| 2.3 | A cancellation arrives for an order in progress | Unmistakably a cancellation |
| 2.4 | A reprint arrives for an order you already made | You can tell it is a reprint, **not a second order** |

> **2.4 is the expensive one.** A reprint mistaken for a new order means food
> made twice. If the "REPRINT" marking is not immediately obvious across a
> counter, it has failed.

## Part 3 · Marking orders ready

| # | Task | Passes when |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Scan the barcode to mark an order ready | Works first time, feedback is immediate |
| 3.2 | Scan the same slip again by accident | **Nothing bad happens.** No error to resolve |
| 3.3 | Scan a slip belonging to a different order | Rejected with a message you understand |
| 3.4 | Scan when the branch has no internet | Still works |

## Part 4 · Under load

| # | Task | Passes when |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Six orders arrive within two minutes | Slips are in a sensible order; nothing lost |
| 4.2 | Printer runs out of paper mid-service | You can tell, and know what to do |
| 4.3 | Printer is switched off and back on | Missing slips arrive; **nothing printed twice unnoticed** |

---

## What the observer records

- Every slip that needed a second look, and which part was unclear
- Every time a participant asked a colleague what something meant
- Whether modifiers were noticed **without prompting**
- Physical problems: print too small, thermal fade, curl, position on the paper
